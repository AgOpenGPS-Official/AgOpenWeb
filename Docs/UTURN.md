# U-turns

Every rule of the U-turn system, with its reasons and its AgOpenGPS references. Read this before changing U-turn code; the short version is in `CLAUDE.md`.


U-turns are planned by `YouTurnStateMachine` (automatic turns at the turn line, and manual
turns from the on-screen buttons) with paths built by `YouTurnCreationService`:
- **The turn line, not the headland**, as AgOpenGPS (`CYouTurn` uses only `turnLine`, `CTurn.BuildTurnLines`): the fence moved in by the U-turn distance from boundary. The automatic turn's trigger, zones and landing checks read it (`GpsPipelineService`: `turnLine`, the turn area of the out-of-bounds test too), with a headland or without, on or off. The headland is for the sections, the hydraulic lift and the drawing, and for the "Headland too narrow" warning (`TickContext.WorkHeadlandLine`). A pass inside the headland band turns like any other (#694: a boundary curve's outer passes).
- Automatic: entry leg, arc, exit leg back to the next track; triggered when the tractor reaches the turn start, with an approach alarm 20 m out.
- The first turn on a track goes the way the work is (`WorkSideForFirstTurn`, the normal and the alternate-skip modes): with a headland, the side whose next pass has at least the landing length (20 m) inside the area it encloses; the pass along the edge the operator started beside has a sliver in it (15 m, J20) or lies in the headland band inside the turn line (J11), and counting it sent the first turn at the edge. Without a work area, or no such pass either side, the turn line decides.
- Rows wider than twice the turn radius (a wide spreader, skipped rows) get AgOpenGPS's wide turn (`CreateCurveWideTurn`): a quarter circle out of the pass, tangent to the turn line, the turn line itself, a quarter circle into the next pass, so the distance from the boundary holds on an angled headland (#544). The omega turn (one shape square to the track) is the fallback when it can't be built.
- Manual: a Dubins path at the configured `UTurnRadius` from 4 m ahead of the tractor to the point abreast on the target pass (an omega when the radius exceeds half the pass offset), laid out along the track's local heading (the curve's heading at the nearest point); the path's first point is the tractor itself. Like AgOpenGPS `BuildManualYouTurn`.
- The state machine's `StatusMessage` is not sent to the web client. What the operator must see is marked `YouTurnEffects.TellOperator` and shown as a toast (`GpsCycleResult.YouTurnNotice` → `ReportFailure`): a manual U-turn that was refused (every way out of `TriggerManual` but a started turn), and a turn that can't be made or was missed, once, with its sound (`SoundMissed`, `SoundCreationFailed`).
- The turn hands back to line guidance with 4 m of arc remaining (`EarlyCompletionLookahead`).
- While a turn is driven (`IsExecuting`) the pass shown is the one it lands on (`GpsPipelineService.TurnTargetPass`): the highlighted line, the tool's band, the guidelines and the pass label, as AgOpenGPS, which moves to the next pass when the turn starts (`CYouTurn.YouTurnTrigger`). The pass worked (`HowManyPathsAway`: the worked-pass mark, the snake order, a turn backed out of, #588) still moves on in `CompleteTurn`.
- A turn that loops (the next pass closer than twice the radius) is moved in to stay inside the turn line, as AgOpenGPS; when the headland is narrower than the loop is deep it leaves its pass inside the headland line, still working, and the status says "Headland too narrow for this U-turn" with how far before the line (`TurnStartInsideHeadlandLine`).
- **One end of a pass** (`PassEnds.Find`): walking the pass from the tractor's point, the first point outside the turn line after the pass was inside, as AgOpenGPS (`CYouTurn.FindCurveTurnPoint`). The state machine's distance to the turn (`DistanceToHeadland`, measured along the pass, the pass and its end cached per pass: `DistanceToPassEnd`), the turn builder's arc start (`YouTurnCreationInput.TurnLineEndIndex`, set by `BuildCreationInput`), the retry with the turn line moved in and the fallback turn all use that end; the pass they walk is `YouTurnCreationService.PassForTurn` (the track offset, then extended). Not a straight ray along the heading (on a curve it left the field early on the outside of a bend, J10) and not the crossing driven in over at the start of a job. A stretch outside the turn line that stays inside the fence, the pass back inside the turn line after it, is driven through, not turned at: the end of a pass is the turn-line crossing that the fence's crossing follows (J19: pass -18 runs along a slanted edge 1.2–2.1 m inside the fence for 26 m with 180 m of field beyond; turned there, it landed 11 m short of the next pass's end and the job stopped; AgOpenGPS turns at the first point outside the turn line). The turn line is a margin for turning, not for driving along an edge. The next pass's end, where the wide turn runs to along the turn line, is found the same way (`FindCurveOutTurnPoint`, `PassEnds.IsDrivenThrough`, `YouTurnCreationInput.IsPointDrivenThrough`): walking the next pass from abreast of the tractor, the first point outside the turn line was taken for its end, so where the next pass runs past a bulge of the fence nearer than the U-turn distance it "ended" there, and the wide turn ran along the turn line back to it (a tester's field with an 8 m front boom, radius 5.4 m, 6.3 m from the boundary, one row skipped: 300 m round a corner, refused as malformed beside a hard fence; `WideTurnNextPassEndTests`: 209 m, 37 m with the bulge driven past, hard or soft). AgOpenGPS finds the real end there too: it walks the turn line from the tractor's crossing in the turn's direction and takes the first segment the next pass crosses (`CYouTurn.FindCurveOutTurnPoint`). When no real turn fits, the simple fallback (a semicircle) is used only if every point of it stays in the field, outside obstacles a turn may not drive through (`LeavesField`, #698); otherwise no turn and the creation-failed sound, as AgOpenGPS (which has no fallback). A hard boundary refuses a turn that would start under or behind the tractor.
- A turn tried again with the turn line moved in, to keep the implement off a hard fence (`CreateTurnPath`'s setback loop, which moves the whole fence in), is seated back from the end of the pass the first try found: walking back from that end, at the moved line's crossing nearest to it (`YouTurnCreationInput.TurnLineEndIndex`, `TurnLineEndOnRetry`). Moved in, the turn line also crossed the pass where the fence comes in beside it, and that first crossing ahead was taken: an 8 m boom near a hard fence turned 260 m before the end of its pass, and the strip it left on the next pass was never worked.
- The next pass must have room at this end: at least 20 m of it inside the cultivated area back from the end ahead (`IsPathLandable` / `LandingLength`), not just any piece of it inside the field; otherwise no turn, and the operator is told why and takes over at the end of the pass: "No U-turn onto the next pass: only 15 m of it at this end, 20 m needed", or, when the next pass runs on but nearer the fence than the U-turn distance after a short piece, "16 m in, it runs 1.0 m from the boundary, 2.0 m needed" (#712; `YouTurnPathingService.NoLandingMessage`; J9 and J18 on Olival, where no tractor fits between the pass and the fence). "End of field reached" is said only when no pass is left in the field. Driving on across a headland gap to the next piece is not done (an enhancement).
- Skip worked passes (the snake order): when the order's next pass has no room at this end, or its turn does not build, the turn goes onto the nearest pass still to do that has room, first on the side the order is going, then on the other side (one turn built per side; a pass at least the skip away before a nearer one; none further than twice the skip's step, else "End of field reached" and the operator decides), never against the side the operator picked with the U-turn arrow (`TurnOntoNearestOpenPass`; AgOpenGPS's `GetNextNotWorkedTrack` only steps outward on the turn's side past worked tracks, so the other side, passes nearer than the skip and the room and build checks are ours); the order is planned again from where it lands. An end where no turn was made is remembered (`_failedEnd`) and tried again every 1.5 s (AgOpenGPS's wait before it builds again, `makeUTurnCounter`), silently, the operator having been told once, and at once when the pass, the direction of travel, the nudge, the order, the field or headland, the skip, the U-turn arrow or a turn setting changes. The normal and alternative modes remember such an end the same way (`TriedJustNow`; the field's stamp also counts the operator's headland and the fence made hard or soft, the key the mode): built again on every cycle, a turn that could not be made (a tractor parked with AutoSteer on) filled a bug report's 2000 log lines in 13 s. A short spell of not looking for a turn (the heading flickering at the edge of the 20° gate) keeps the end; it is forgotten when a turn is planned or driven, or after 1.5 s of not looking. Test: `FailedEndRetryTests`. The order is also planned again when the headland line or the outer boundary changes (`SnakeFieldStamp`). In the headland the snake looks for its next turn only heading out of the field (no cultivated ground ahead on the pass): a turn that ends in the headland leaves the tractor facing the field on its new pass, which is not the end of that pass.
- An open curve whose ends face each other (a boundary curve made the long way round, A and B with an opening between them): A and B are the line's ends, no run-on past them, and the opening is a virtual boundary (`CurveEndOpening`, a strip of twice the U-turn radius plus the distance from boundary past A and B): the U-turn turns there into the next pass as at the field's edge. A turn that would hand over past the end of the next pass's line (the inner passes, whose two ends meet in a V, with a skip) is not made (`TurnLanding.PastLineEnd`). Past an open curve's end with no turn to take over, AutoSteer goes off ("past end of curve", AgOpenGPS `CABCurve`) instead of steering back (#694).
- The U-turn arrow in the snake order: the side the operator picked holds whichever way the order goes (`operatorSide` in `HandleSnakeCreation`). `TakeDirectionSwap` answers only whether the arrow asks for the other side, and an arrow pointing the order's way was used up with the side left open, so with no turn onto the order's pass the nearest pass was looked for on both sides and the turn went against the arrow. An end where no turn was made is tried again with the arrow's side, in every mode: the try took the arrow, and the retry 1.5 s later turned the other way (`TriedJustNow` gives it back from `HeldDirectionLeft`, kept until a turn starts, #577). Tests: `SnakeOpenPassTests`, `FailedEndRetryTests`.

Key parameters:
- `UTurnDistanceFromBoundary` - the turn line's distance in from the fence. With a soft outer boundary the turn keeps the tractor inside the turn line, not the implement, as AgOpenGPS (`CTurn.BuildTurnLines` moves the fence in by this distance alone). With a hard outer boundary (ours: AgOpenGPS has no hard boundary) it is also the gap the implement keeps from the fence: `CreateTurnPath` sweeps the implement's corners along the smoothed turn (`ImplementSweptPath`, `TurnClearance`) and, where one comes nearer the fence than this distance, moves the turn line in by the shortfall (0.5 m at least, 30 m at most) and builds the turn again; when none clears, no turn (`ClearanceBlocked`) and no fallback. A manual U-turn starts at the tractor and can't be moved in, so it is refused instead (`ImplementClearsHardBoundary`). The implement is already counted: on a hard fence the value is the gap wanted between implement and fence, not the implement's overhang plus a gap (that counts the overhang twice, and turns that fit are moved in or refused)
- `UTurnSmoothing` (default 14; 1 = none) - passes of a 3-point average (`TurnPathSmoothing.Smooth`) over the simple fallback turn (`SimpleFallback`) and the manual U-turn (`CreateManualArcPath`) only. The automatic turn does not read it: it is smoothed once with AgOpenGPS's fixed `SmoothYouTurn(path, 6)` (6 points, one pass, a U turn, not a K-turn), as AgOpenGPS (`Position.designer.cs`: `yt.SmoothYouTurn(6)` when `uTurnStyle == 0`). AgOpenGPS loads, shows and saves `setAS_uTurnSmoothing`, but none of its turns reads `CYouTurn.uTurnSmoothing`
- `UTurnRadius` - the operator's turning radius (never filled in from the tractor)
- `UTurnExtension` - the legs' length into and out of the arc
- The headland's width plays no part in the turn: with a headland narrower than the turn is deep, the turn leaves its pass inside the headland line, still working ("Headland too narrow for this U-turn").
