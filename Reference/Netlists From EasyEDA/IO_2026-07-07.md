# I/O sheet — netlist (to-wire, real designators)

> Wiring list for the **off-board I/O & protection** sheet, keyed to the actual EasyEDA designators
> from `I-O-copy_2026-07-07.net`. Sheet exported **unwired** (component list only) — this file is the
> to-wire target. Blocks: analog sensor entry (WAS/current ESD), digital switch inputs (2-stage
> protection), **direct** steering-control outputs (AOG-standard, no optos). Cross-sheet rails:
> `5V_MAIN`, `+3V3`, `GND`. Off-board signals are cross-sheet labels to the **ATS-26 connector (Page 7)**.
>
> **Steering = direct GPIO drive** (matches MD13S / IBT-2 / Cytron, which take low-freq CMOS PWM+DIR
> and share ground) — the opto bank (U13 6N137, U14/U15 LTV-357, C74) is **deleted**; R27–R29 stay as
> series resistors. See fix #6.
>
> **Pinouts verified against datasheets:** SRV05-4 (SOT-23-6: 1/3/4/6 = I/O, 2 = GND, 5 = VCC);
> ESD9B5V (2-pin bidir); SMAJ16A (2-pin unidir, cathode = band).

## Fix / confirm

1. ✅ **Diodes renumbered** — D9→D13, D10→D14, D11→D15, D12→D16, D13→D17 (confirmed in wired export).
   No longer collide with the Serial sheet.
2. ✅ **Digital-input conditioning added** (dry-contact-to-ground) — per input: series R 1 kΩ
   (R58/R60), pull-up 10 kΩ to +3V3 (R59/R61), filter 100 nF to GND (C72/C73). Joins `SW_x_IN`↔
   `SW_x`. Open = HIGH, closed = 0.3 V LOW; a 12 V miswire is limited to ~8 mA into the SRV05-4 clamp.
4. **ISENSE path reserved** — `ISENSE_IN` clamps at entry but the MCU side (`ISENSE_ADC` = U7.19) has
   no divider/link yet. Resolve the current-sense chain (divider R_CS) when that feature is finalized.
6. ⚠ **Steering = Option A direct drive** — **delete U13 (6N137), U14/U15 (LTV-357), C74** from the
   sheet + BOM. Rewire: PA8→R27→`STEER_PWM`, PB1→R28→`STEER_DIR`, PB0→R29→`STEER_EN_OUT` (R27–R29
   kept as 330 Ω series). Driver ground shares board GND. Frees `STEER_VCC`/`STEER_RTN` → ATS-26 no
   longer over pins (SW_REMOTE stays; a freed pin becomes steer/sig GND).
