# I/O sheet — netlist (to-wire, real designators)

> Wiring list for the **off-board I/O & protection** sheet, keyed to the actual EasyEDA designators
> from `I-O-copy_2026-07-07.net`. Sheet exported **unwired** (component list only) — this file is the
> to-wire target. Blocks: analog sensor entry (WAS/current ESD), digital switch inputs (2-stage
> protection), isolated steering-control optos. Cross-sheet rails: `5V_MAIN`, `+3V3`, `GND`.
> Off-board signals are cross-sheet labels to the combined **ATS-26 connector on Page 7**.
>
> **Pinouts verified against datasheets:** SRV05-4 (SOT-23-6: 1/3/4/6 = I/O, 2 = GND, 5 = VCC);
> 6N137 (2 = anode, 3 = cathode, 5 = GND, 6 = VO, 7 = VE, 8 = VCC); LTV-357 (1 = anode, 2 = cathode,
> 3 = emitter, 4 = collector); ESD9B5V (2-pin bidir); SMAJ16A (2-pin unidir, cathode = band).

### Isolated amp-side (→ connector Page 7; separate ground domain)

| Net | Nodes |
|---|---|
| `STEER_VCC` | U13.8 (VCC), U13.7 (VE), C74.1 → connector (amp supply) |
| `STEER_PWM` | U13.6 (VO) → connector |
| `STEER_DIR` | U14.4 (collector) → connector |
| `STEER_EN_OUT` | U15.4 (collector) → connector |
| `STEER_RTN` | U13.5 (GND), C74.2, U14.3 (emitter), U15.3 (emitter) → connector — **isolated, NOT board GND** |

> U13 pins 1, 4 = NC. VE (7) tied to VCC (8) = output always enabled. **6N137 needs a 0.1 µF bypass
> across pin 8→pin 5 (isolated side) — not on sheet, see fix #3.** LTV-357 collectors are pulled up
> by the amp's rail (open-collector) — no supply needed on our board.

## Fix / confirm

1. ✅ **Diodes renumbered** — D9→D13, D10→D14, D11→D15, D12→D16, D13→D17 (confirmed in wired export).
   No longer collide with the Serial sheet.
2. ✅ **Digital-input conditioning added** (dry-contact-to-ground) — per input: series R 1 kΩ
   (R58/R60), pull-up 10 kΩ to +3V3 (R59/R61), filter 100 nF to GND (C72/C73). Joins `SW_x_IN`↔
   `SW_x`. Open = HIGH, closed = 0.3 V LOW; a 12 V miswire is limited to ~8 mA into the SRV05-4 clamp.
3. ✅ **6N137 output bypass added** — C74 (100 nF) across U13.8 (STEER_VCC) → U13.5 (STEER_RTN),
   isolated side. LTV-357 optos need none.
4. **ISENSE path reserved** — `ISENSE_IN` clamps at entry but the MCU side (`ISENSE_ADC` = U7.19) has
   no divider/link yet. Resolve the current-sense chain (divider R_CS) when that feature is finalized.
5. **Isolation** — keep `STEER_RTN` + amp-side nets a separate copper island; the optos are the only
   bridge to the board domain. Confirm opto LED drive polarity (STM sources: high = LED on).
